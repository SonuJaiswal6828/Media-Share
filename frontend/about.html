About MediaShare

A secure media sharing platform built on a simple idea — you should control who sees your content, and for how long.

📁

The Project

MediaShare is a full-stack platform where admins organize photos and documents into groups and sections, and share them with others using temporary, revocable access.

Every access grant creates a 1-hour session with a unique token. The admin can approve or reject requests and revoke any active session at any moment — giving complete control over who has access to what.

Design Philosophy

MediaShare is built with a focus on security, simplicity, and control.

No clutter. No confusion. Just the features you need to share media safely — with complete visibility into who has access and when it expires.
